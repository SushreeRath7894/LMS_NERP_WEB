Preparation of reagent:

1. Oxalic Acid: 1% w/v

2. Dilute sodium bicarbonate solution

3. 2,6 Dichlorophenol Indophenol Solution: Weight approximately 0.05 g dye in a 100 ml beaker . Dispers in boiling hot water , transfer into a 250 ml volumetric flask through whatman filter paper no.41.Add more boiling hot water so that final volume is approximately 100 ml. Cool the solution under tap water. Adjust thr pH to 8.0 using dilute sodium bicarbonate solution. Make upto the mark. Keep in refrigerated condition (concentration of dye is about 0.25 mg/ml).

4. Standard ascorbic acid (AR grade) solution :

i. Stock solution (1 mg/ml): Weigh accurately, approximately 100 mg ascorbic acid in a beaker (100 ml). Dissolve it in small quantity of oxalic acid solution transfer quantitatively into a 100 ml volumetric flask and make upto the mark with oxalic acid solution.

ii. Working standard solution (0.5mg/ml): Pipette out 50 ml of ascorbic acid stock solution into 100 ml volumetric flask and make upto the mark using oxalic acid solution.

Method of Standardization:

Fill the burette with the dye solution.End point to be determined visually. Run blank determination using similar quantity of oxalic acid except vitamin C. To an erlenmeyer flask (250 ml) add approximately50 ml oxalic acid solution. Pipette out 5 ml of ascorbic acid working standard solution. Titrate with dye solution taken in the burette slowly till pink color appears as end point and persist for 15 sec. Repeat titration two more times to have triplicate values Calculate dye equivalent for each values and take average for vitamin C calculation.
