Preparation of reagent:

1. Neutral Alcohol(90 % V/V):Add 25 ml of distilled water to 475 ml of ethyl alcohol(absolute).To this solution add 2-3 drops of phenolphthalein indicator solution and just sufficient 0.05N sodium hydroxide to produce faint pink color.

2. Phenolphthalein Indicator(1% W/V): Dissolve 1gm phenolphthalein indicator in 100ml of ethanol-distilled water mixture in the ratio of 1:1.

3. Alcoholic Sodium Hydroxide(0.05N): To 250ml of ethyl alcohol absolute,AR grade add 2ml of 25% solution of NaOH

4. Sodium Hydroxide Solution:25% W/V

5. Benzoic Acid

Method of Standardization:

Dissolve about 200mg of benzoic acid, accurately weighed, in 10ml of neutral alcohol solution and 2ml of distilled water. Add 2 drops of phenolphthalein indicator solution and titrate with the alcoholic sodium hydroxide solution until a permanent pale pink color is produced.
