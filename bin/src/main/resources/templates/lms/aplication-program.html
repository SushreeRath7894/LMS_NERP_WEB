About the Program

The Master of Technology (M.Tech) in Computer Science is a two-year full-time program designed to provide students with advanced knowledge and skills in computer science and engineering. The program combines rigorous coursework with hands-on projects and research opportunities.

Key Features
  • Comprehensive curriculum covering advanced topics in computer science
  • Opportunity to specialize in areas like AI, Machine Learning, or Cloud Computing
  • State-of-the-art labs and research facilities
  • Industry collaborations and internship opportunities
  • Expert faculty with research and industry experience
  • Thesis option for research-oriented students
Learning Outcomes

Upon completion of the program, students will be able to:

  • Design and implement complex software systems
  • Apply advanced algorithms and data structures to solve computational problems
  • Conduct research in specialized areas of computer science
  • Work effectively in teams and communicate technical concepts clearly
  • Understand and apply ethical principles in computing practice

Program Structure

The M.Tech in Computer Science program consists of core courses, elective courses, and a thesis or project component. The program requires completion of 64 credits over four semesters.

Core Courses
  • Advanced Algorithms
  • Advanced Database Systems
  • Computer Networks
  • Research Methodology
  • Distributed Systems
Elective Courses
Artificial Intelligence Track
  • Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Computer Vision
Systems Track
  • Cloud Computing
  • Big Data Analytics
  • Internet of Things
  • Cyber Security
Thesis/Project

Students must complete either a research thesis or a capstone project in their chosen area of specialization during the final semester.
