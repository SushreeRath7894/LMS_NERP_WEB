Alcoholic NAOH(0.05M):
Preparation Of reagent:
  1. Neutral Alcohol(90%v/v):bAdd 25 ml of distilled water to 475 ml of ethyl alcohol (absolute). To this solution add 2-3 drops of phenolphthalein indicator solution and just sufficient 0.05N sodium hydroxide to produce faint pink color.
  2. Phenolphthalein Indicator(1% w/v): Dissolve 1 g of phenolphthalein in 100 ml of ethyl alcohol(absolute)
  3. Alcoholic Sodium Hydroxide(0.05N): To 250 ml of ethyl alcohol absolute,AR grade add 2 ml of 25% solution of NaOH.
  4. Sodium Hydroxide solution: 25% w/v
  5. Benzoic Acid 25% w/v
Method Of Standardization:
  1. Dissolve about 200mg of benzoic acid,accurately weighed, in 10 ml of neutral alcohol solution and 2 ml of distilled water.Add 2 drops of phenolphthalein indicator solution and titrate with the alcoholic sodium hydroxide solution until a permanent pale pink color is produced.Calculate the normality of alcoholic sodium hydroxide as described below.

  1. P=Purity pf Benzoic acid in % W/W and 122.12=Molecular weight of benzoic acid
  2. Strength of Dye has to be mentioned up to 4th decimal place.
  3. Three titrations have to be carried out for standardization & take the avg. of two close reading (difference between the repetitions to be within 0.5%)
