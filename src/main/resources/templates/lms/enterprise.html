Enterprise Skilling for Global Tech Giant

Release V.0.0.0

Organization

A USD 13.25 billion company with 2,27,000+ professionals across 60 countries. It operates through the following segments: Software Services; Information Technology (IT) Services and Business Process Outsourcing Services. The Software Services segment provides application development and maintenance, enterprise application, next generation software as a service application services, engineering, research and development. The IT Services segment provides management services involving customer's information technology assets.

Context

Towards the end of 2022 it was felt that young workforce joining the organization and working across different geographies needed to be upskilled in ITIL 4 to ensure that they were upto-date on the latest trends and practices due to upgradation of the IT service Management approach from a “Process Driven” approach to a “Value Stream” based approach. The need was to train and certify upwards of 2000 IT professionals in the period Early Jan 2023 to March 2024.

Solution

Key consultants at Ducis were identified from a training delivery and operational coordination perspective to ensure:

  • Quality Delivery
  • Monitoring Student Progress
  • Compliance to Certification standards
  • Extensive reporting to ensure effective results.

    • Training deliveries were staggered over time through extensive online support including online e-learning, live learning by accredited professionals, and daily operational reports delivered mid-day for ensuring effective training delivery.

Result

By May 2024, Ducis had assisted the successful training and accreditation of 2500 + ITIL 4 Foundation certified professionals in 8 countries besides India in this organization.
