Our Refund Policy

At Ducis Group, we strive to ensure you are completely satisfied with your learning experience. If a course does not meet your expectations, we offer a straightforward refund policy to provide peace of mind.

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all paid courses purchased through our platform. If you are not satisfied with a course, you may request a full refund within 30 days from the date of purchase, provided the following conditions are met:

  • The refund request is submitted within 30 calendar days of the purchase date.
  • You have not completed more than 50% of the course content, as tracked by our platform.
  • The course has not been downloaded or accessed offline in its entirety.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  • Contact our support team via the Contact Us .
  • Provide your order details, including the course name and purchase date.
  • Explain the reason for your refund request (this helps us improve our offerings).

Once your request is received, our team will review it and process the refund within 5-7 business days. Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used during purchase.

Non-Refundable Items

Certain products or services are non-refundable, including:

  • Free courses or promotional offers.
  • Courses where more than 50% of the content has been completed.
  • Digital downloads or offline course materials accessed in full.
  • Subscription-based services after the initial 30-day period.

Additional Information

If you encounter any issues with a course, we encourage you to reach out to our support team before requesting a refund. We’re here to help resolve any concerns and ensure you have the best learning experience possible.

For region-specific refund policies or payment method restrictions, please check with our support team, as terms may vary based on your location.
