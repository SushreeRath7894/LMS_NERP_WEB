The Ticket Management Module is a system designed to track, manage, and resolve customer or internal issues efficiently. It helps in organizing requests, assigning tasks, and monitoring the status of tickets from creation to closure

To create a new ticket, click on the "Create Ticket" button, fill in the required details like subject, description, priority, and category, then click "Submit." The ticket will be logged into the system and assigned to the appropriate team.

Yes, when creating or updating a ticket, you can select a specific team member from the "Assignee" dropdown list based on availability or expertise.

The typical statuses include: Open: The ticket has been created but not yet assigned. In Progress: The ticket is being worked on. On Hold: The ticket is paused, awaiting further information. Resolved: The issue has been addressed but awaits confirmation. Closed: The ticket is completed and verified.

Go to the "My Tickets" section, where you’ll find a list of all your tickets with their current status. You can also click on a ticket to view detailed updates and comments.

Yes, if the issue persists, you can reopen a closed ticket by selecting the ticket and clicking the "Reopen" button, providing a reason for reopening.

When creating a ticket, you can select the priority level (Low, Medium, High, Critical) based on the urgency and impact of the issue. This helps the support team address tickets more effectively.

Categorization helps in routing the ticket to the right department or specialist, ensuring faster resolution. It also aids in generating accurate reports and analyzing common issues.

Yes, you will receive automatic email or in-app notifications whenever there’s an update on your ticket, such as status changes, comments from the support team, or resolution notices.

The system has built-in escalation rules. If a ticket is not addressed within the defined SLA (Service Level Agreement), it will automatically escalate to higher management for prompt action.

Yes, you can upload relevant files, screenshots, or documents while creating or updating a ticket to provide additional context to the support team.
