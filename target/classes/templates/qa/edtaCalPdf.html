Preparation of reagent: (as per C-6446)

1. EDTA (0.01M): Dissolve 4.0g EDTA in 1 L distilled water.

2. Sodium Hydroxide (5N): Dissolve 200g NaOH in 1 L of distilled water.

3. Solochrome Dark Blue solution : 1% W/V in distilled water

4. Hydrochloric Acid: 10% V/V in distilled water

Method of Standardization: (as per C-6446)

Weigh 30 mg of CaCO3 (previously dried at 1040C for 2 hr) in 250 ml concal flask.Dissolve using Hydrochloric acid (10 % V/V).Add 3 ml of 5 N NaOH solution. Stand for 2-3 min. Add 50 ml distilled water and pinch of Hydrxynapthol blue.Titrate against EDTA solution with drop wise addition till the appearance of blue colour.
