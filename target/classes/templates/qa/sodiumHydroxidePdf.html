Preparation of reagent:

1. 0.1 N NaOH: Dissolve 4.0g of NaOH pellets in 200 ml of distilled water. Cool and dilute to 1000 ml with distilled water.

2. Phenolphthalein Indicator : Dissolve 1 gm. Phenolphthalein indicator in 100 ml of ethanol -distilled water mixture in the ratio of 1:1 .

Method of Standardization:

Weigh 0.1 g potassium Hydrogen Phthalate (KHP) (Previously dried at 105 0C for 3 hr.)in a dried conical flask. Add 50 ml water and dissolve by swirling. Add 2 drops of phenolphthalein indicator and titrate with standard Sodium Hydroxide Solution, till the end point pink colour persists for at least 5s. Calculate the Normality of sodium hydroxide as given below.
