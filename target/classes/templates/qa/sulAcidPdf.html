Preparation of reagent:

1. 0.1 N Sulphuric Acid: Pour slowly 2.8ml conc. Sulphuric acid into 200ml of distilled water. Cool and dilute to 1000ml with distilled water.

2. Phenolphthalein Indicator: Dissolve 1gm phenolphthalein indicator in 100ml of ethanol-distilled water mixture in the ratio of 1:1.

Method of Standardization:

Transfer 10ml of H2SO4 into a conical flask. Add 2-3 drops of phenolphthalein indicator and titrate with standardized NaOH, till the pink color of phenolphthalein appears by addition of a single drop of NaOH. Calculate the normality of sulphuric acid as below.
