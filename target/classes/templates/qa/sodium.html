Sodium Hydroxide:
Preparation Of reagent:
  1. 0.1 N NaOH: Dissolve 4.Og of NaOH pellets in 200ml of distilled water.Cool and dilute to 1000ml with distilled water.
  2. Phenolphthalein Indicator: Dissolve 1 gm. Phenolphthalein indicatore in 100ml of ethanol -distilled water mixture in the ratio of 1.1.
Method Of Standardization:
  1. Weight 0.1 g Potassium Hydrogen Phthalate (KHP) (Previously dried at 105 OC for 3hr.)in a dried conical flask.Add 50ml water and dissolve by swirling.Add 2 drops of phenolphalein indicator and titrate with standard Sodium Hydroxide Solution,till the end point pink colour persists for at least 5s. Calculate the Normality of sodium hydroxide as given below.

  1. Normality of reagent has to be mentioned up to 4th decimal place.
  2. Three titrations have to be carried out for standardization & take the average of two close reading(difference between the repetitions to be within 0.5%)
  3. P=Purity of Potassium Hydrogen Phthalate & 204.22=Molecular wt of Potassium Hydrogen Phthalate
