NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing and the mutual covenants and promises contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the Employee inter-alia agrees to certain terms and conditions as contained herein.

  1. Confidentiality obligation: "Confidential Information" encompasses all information pertaining to the Company's business that is either unknown to actual or potential competitors or is proprietary to the Company, regardless of whether it is technical in nature. This includes, but is not limited to, inventions, disclosures, processes, systems, methods, formulas, devices, patents, patent applications, trademarks, intellectual property, instruments, materials, products, patterns, compilations, programs, techniques, sequences, designs, research and development activities, plans, specifications, computer programs, source codes, works of authorship, production costs, pricing or other financial data, sales volumes, promotional strategies, marketing plans, customer or personnel lists, supplier lists, business plans, business opportunities, financial statements, and any clientrelated information. Client-related information includes, without limitation, client data, ideas, intellectual property rights, or any other information shared by the client with the Company.


  2. Non-Disclosure - The Employee agrees to maintain strict confidentiality regarding all Company Confidential Information and will not disclose, use, copy, publish, summarize, or remove such information from the Company premises, whether knowingly or unknowingly, except under the following conditions: (a) as required to perform assigned duties as a Company employee, and (b) after employment termination, only with specific written authorization from the Company. Furthermore, the Employee undertakes not to utilize the Confidential Information in any manner that could harm the Company's interests.

  3. Scope of Confidentiality Obligations: The Employee's confidentiality obligations under this NDA extend to all Confidential Information, including information disclosed in connection with employment prior to the execution of this NDA. However, these obligations shall not apply to information that:

    1. Was already in the Employee's possession or known to the Employee, without any obligation of confidentiality, before being disclosed by the Company;
    2. Becomes publicly available through no fault or action of the Employee;
    3. Is lawfully obtained by the Employee from a source other than the Company, without any obligation of confidentiality; or
    4. Must be disclosed by the Employee pursuant to a court order or a requirement from an administrative or governmental agency, provided that the Employee promptly notifies the Company in writing of such an order or requirement and allows the Company the opportunity to contest or seek a protective order.

  4. Protection of Third-Party Information: The Employee shall safeguard and maintain the confidentiality of proprietary information belonging to customers, vendors, employees, shareholders, and other parties associated with the Company, treating such information with the same care as Company Confidential Information. The Employee further agrees not to use or disclose any confidential, trade secret, or proprietary information of a previous employer or any other entity during their employment with the Company or otherwise. Additionally, the Employee will not bring any unpublished documents or other property belonging to a former employer or any other individual or organization onto the Company’s premises without obtaining prior written consent from the former employer or the respective entity.

  5. Company Property: All works, programs, papers, records, data, notes, drawings, files, documents, samples, devices, products, equipment, and other materials, including any copies in any format, translations into other languages, and intellectual property, related to the Company's business and created or possessed by the Employee during their employment, shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company. Upon termination of employment, the Employee agrees to promptly return all such materials to the Company.

  6. Ownership of Inventions and works of Authorship: All inventions, ideas, designs, circuits, schematics, formulas, algorithms, trade secrets, works of authorship, developments, processes, techniques, improvements, and related know-how (collectively referred to as "Inventions and Works") that arise from the Employee's work, whether individually or collaboratively, on behalf of the Company, or through access to the Company's Confidential Information or property, or created during the Employee's service with the Company, shall be the exclusive property of the Company. This applies whether or not such Inventions and Works are patentable, copyrightable, or otherwise protected under intellectual property laws and includes all translation rights.
    To the fullest extent permitted by law, such Inventions and Works shall be considered "works made for hire." The Employee irrevocably assigns and agrees to assign to the Company or its designee all rights, title, and interest in these Inventions and Works, including rights to obtain, register, and enforce patents, copyrights, and other intellectual property protections. The Employee further agrees to promptly disclose in writing to the designated Company representative or immediate supervisor any Inventions and Works created, authored, or reduced to practice during their employment. Additionally, for a period of five years following termination of employment, the Employee will assist the Company, at its expense, in obtaining and enforcing patents, copyrights, and other forms of intellectual property protection for such Inventions and Works.
The Employee acknowledges and agrees that the assignment of Inventions and Works described herein is worldwide, perpetual, and irrevocable. The Employee further agrees that they have no rights to reclaim or revert any Inventions and Works, even if the Company chooses not to exploit them. The Employee permanently waives all paternity, integrity, moral, special, or similar rights that may vest in them at the time of creating any Inventions and Works. This waiver extends to the Employee's legal heirs, who shall also have no such rights in any Inventions and Works created by the Employee. The Employee agrees to provide any necessary assistance, at the Company's request and expense, to confirm and formalize this waiver of paternity, integrity, moral, special, or similar rights associated with the Inventions and Works.

  1. Prior Contracts: The Employee represents that there are no other contracts to assign inventions or works that are now in existence between him and any other person or entity. The Employee further represents that he has no other employment(s), consultancies or undertakings which would restrict and impair his performance of this NDA.


  2.
